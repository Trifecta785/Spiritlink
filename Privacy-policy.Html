SpiritLink Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

SpiritLink respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the SpiritLink mobile application.

Information We Collect

SpiritLink may process information that users voluntarily provide while using features of the application. Information collected by the app will be limited to what is necessary to provide and improve its features.

Device and Technical Information

Certain technical information may be processed automatically by the app or services used to operate it, such as device type, operating system, app performance information, and diagnostic information.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to operate SpiritLink, provide app functionality, troubleshoot problems, maintain security, and improve the user experience.

Sharing of Information

SpiritLink does not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate or maintain the application.

Children's Privacy

SpiritLink is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information associated with SpiritLink. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as SpiritLink develops or its features change. Updates will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be directed to the SpiritLink developer through the contact information provided on the application's Google Play listing.
